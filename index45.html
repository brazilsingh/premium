The problem

Your AI agents are already running. Can you prove what they did?

AI agents are making decisions inside your business right now. Screening applicants, routing claims, answering customers, moving data. Most organisations cannot say what those agents did, which touched personal data, or what happened when an automation failed silently.

No visibility

Agents run, configurations change, conversations happen. Nobody sees it until something breaks.

No evidence

EU AI Act and ISO 42001 documentation assembled by hand, after the fact, in the week the auditor arrives.

No control

Nothing stops an agent doing something it shouldn't. You find out afterwards, from someone else.

EU AI Act & ISO 42001

Built for the regulation, aligned to the standard

Define your AI systems, including the connected infrastructure the Act brings into scope. A fundamental rights impact assessment drives the risk classification: high-risk, limited-risk or prohibited. Map the harm scenarios, the operational triggers, and the groups those harms would fall on.

Systems that touch hiring, credit, healthcare or public services are high-risk by default under Annex III. @Human generates that reporting from records it already holds, aligned to ISO 42001, the international standard for AI management systems.

Who does it protect?

Applicants filtered by a screening model. Patients triaged by an AI assistant. Children affected by content recommendations. Older adults dependent on automated care decisions. Ethnic and racial minorities assessed by algorithmic systems. The EU AI Act exists because these decisions have consequences, and someone has to be accountable.

FAQ

AI governance, answered

What is an AI governance platform?

An AI governance platform lets an organisation build, monitor and prove the compliance of its AI agents and automations. @Human logs every agent action, tracks failures as incidents, runs AI risk management, and maps each AI system to the EU AI Act and ISO 42001, so compliance is demonstrable rather than assumed.

Does @Human support EU AI Act compliance?

@Human classifies AI systems as high-risk, limited-risk or prohibited, runs a fundamental rights impact assessment, maps harm scenarios to affected groups, and generates the documentation the Act requires, aligned to the ISO 42001 AI management standard.

How does @Human relate to Corteza?

Corteza is Planet Crust's platform for building enterprise applications safely, in production since 2019 and available today. @Human is our AI governance platform. Both are built on the same principle: systems that are safe by design, with guard rails and integrated security.

Can @Human be self-hosted for data sovereignty?

Yes. @Human can be self-hosted on your own infrastructure or run in an EU cloud region. Your logs, audit records and sensitive data stay where you put them, with your choice of LLM including Mistral.
